INTRODUCTION TO ANATOMY & PHYSIOLOGY

🔹 Definition

Anatomy is the branch of biology that studies the structure of the human body and the relationship between its different parts.


Physiology is the branch of biology that studies the functions of the human body and explains how organs and body systems work together to maintain life.

🔹 Relationship Between Anatomy & Physiology

Anatomy and physiology are closely related because structure determines function.


Example:

The lungs contain millions of tiny air sacs called alveoli, providing a large surface area for efficient gas exchange.

🔹 Levels of Structural Organization

1. Chemical Level (Atoms & Molecules)

2. Cellular Level (Cells)

3. Tissue Level (Groups of Similar Cells)

4. Organ Level (Heart, Lungs, Liver, etc.)

5. Organ System Level

6. Organism Level (Complete Human Body)

🔹 Major Organ Systems

• Integumentary System

• Skeletal System

• Muscular System

• Nervous System

• Endocrine System

• Cardiovascular System

• Lymphatic (Immune) System

• Respiratory System

• Digestive System

• Urinary System

• Reproductive System

🔹 Homeostasis

Homeostasis is the ability of the body to maintain a stable internal environment despite internal and external changes.


Examples:

• Body Temperature

• Blood Glucose Level

• Blood Pressure

🔹 Importance of Anatomy & Physiology

• Helps understand the structure and function of the human body.

• Forms the foundation of medicine, nursing and allied health sciences.

• Assists in diagnosing, preventing and treating diseases.

• Promotes healthy living by understanding body responses to exercise, nutrition and illness.
