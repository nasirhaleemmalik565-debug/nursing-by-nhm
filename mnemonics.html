Mnemonics
IMPORTANT MNEMONICS
1. ABC (Primary Survey)
Airway • Breathing • Circulation
2. CAB (Adult BLS)
Compressions • Airway • Breathing
3. PASS (Fire Extinguisher)
Pull • Aim • Squeeze • Sweep
4. RACE (Fire Safety)
Rescue • Alarm • Confine • Extinguish / Evacuate
5. FAST (Stroke Recognition)
Face • Arm Weakness • Speech Difficulty • Time
6. AVPU (Level of Consciousness)
Alert • Voice • Pain • Unresponsive
7. GCS (Glasgow Coma Scale)
Eye Opening • Verbal Response • Motor Response
8. SBAR (Clinical Communication)
Situation • Background • Assessment • Recommendation
9. SOAP (Clinical Documentation)
Subjective • Objective • Assessment • Plan
10. SAMPLE (Patient History)
Signs/Symptoms • Allergies • Medications • Past History • Last Meal • Events
11. AMPLE (Trauma History)
Allergies • Medications • Past History • Last Meal • Events
12. OPQRST (Pain Assessment)
Onset • Provocation/Palliation • Quality • Region/Radiation • Severity • Time
13. DCAP-BTLS (Trauma Assessment)
Deformity • Contusion • Abrasion • Puncture • Burns • Tenderness • Laceration • Swelling
14. PEARL (Pupil Assessment)
Pupils • Equal • And • Reactive to • Light
15. DOPE (ET Tube Troubleshooting)
Displacement • Obstruction • Pneumothorax • Equipment Failure
16. RICE (Soft Tissue Injury)
Rest • Ice • Compression • Elevation
17. PRICE (Soft Tissue Injury)
Protection • Rest • Ice • Compression • Elevation
18. APGAR (Newborn Assessment)
Appearance • Pulse • Grimace • Activity • Respiration
19. TORCH (Congenital Infections)
Toxoplasmosis • Other • Rubella • Cytomegalovirus • Herpes Simplex Virus
20. AEIOU (Altered Consciousness)
Alcohol • Epilepsy • Insulin • Overdose • Uremia
21. Cushing's Triad (Raised ICP)
Hypertension • Bradycardia • Irregular Respiration
22. CHNOPS (Major Elements)
Carbon • Hydrogen • Nitrogen • Oxygen • Phosphorus • Sulfur
23. MONA (Traditional ACS Mnemonic)
Morphine • Oxygen • Nitroglycerin • Aspirin
24. PQRST (Symptom Assessment)
Provocation • Quality • Region/Radiation • Severity • Time
25. RPM (Respiratory Assessment)
Rate • Pattern • Movement
