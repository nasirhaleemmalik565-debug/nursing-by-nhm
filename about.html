📖 About

Nursing by NHM is an independent educational platform created to support nursing students in academic learning, university examinations, and competitive nursing exam preparation. We are committed to providing reliable, easy-to-understand, and high-quality learning resources that help students learn with confidence.

🎯 Our Mission

To make nursing education simple, accessible, organized, and effective by delivering quality learning resources that inspire every nursing student to learn, grow, and succeed.

✨ What We Offer

📚 Subject-wise Notes
Comprehensive and easy-to-understand notes covering all major nursing subjects.

💎 NHM Pearls
High-yield facts, mnemonics, and quick revision points for nursing exams.

❓ Daily MCQs
Practice fresh nursing MCQs every day with instant answers and explanations.

🎯 Mock Tests
Exam-oriented mock tests designed to improve preparation and confidence.

🛠️ Nursing Tools
Useful clinical calculators and converters including BMI, EDD, Drug Dose, IV Flow Rate, Unit Converter, and more.

📰 Latest Updates
Stay informed with the latest nursing guidelines, health programmes, recruitment notifications, and exam updates.

📄 Previous Year Papers
Practice previous year question papers to understand exam patterns and improve performance.

💚 Favorites
Save your favorite notes, pearls, and MCQs for quick access and revision anytime.
