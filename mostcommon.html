🏆 Most Common Facts

🔹 Most common congenital heart disease — Ventricular Septal Defect (VSD)

🔹 Most common cyanotic congenital heart disease — Tetralogy of Fallot

🔹 Most common cause of urinary tract infection — Escherichia coli (E. coli)

🔹 Most common cause of nephrotic syndrome in children — Minimal Change Disease

🔹 Most common type of shock — Septic Shock

🔹 Most common site of ectopic pregnancy — Ampulla of the Fallopian Tube

🔹 Most common organism causing pneumonia — Streptococcus pneumoniae

🔹 Most common organism causing tuberculosis — Mycobacterium tuberculosis

🔹 Most common valve affected in rheumatic heart disease — Mitral Valve

🔹 Most common primary bone tumor — Osteochondroma

🔹 Most common malignant bone tumor — Osteosarcoma

🔹 Most common leukemia in adults — Chronic Lymphocytic Leukemia (CLL)

🔹 Most common leukemia in children — Acute Lymphoblastic Leukemia (ALL)

🔹 Most common type of anemia worldwide — Iron Deficiency Anemia

🔹 Most common endocrine disorder — Diabetes Mellitus
